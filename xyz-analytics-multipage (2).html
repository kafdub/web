Page 02 — Process

A four-step path from question to answer.

No black boxes. You see a written plan before we build, working output every week, and full documentation at handover.

STEP 01

Discover

We sit with your data, your tools, and your team to understand the actual question — not just the requested report.

STEP 02

Design

A short written plan: data sources, transformations, outputs, and the checks that prove the numbers are right.

What does "from €12,000" depend on?

Project price is set by the written scope: number of data sources, complexity of transformations, and how much validation the outputs need. You see the fixed figure before we start — the "from" price covers a typical single-pipeline or single-dashboard build.

Do you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We build in your stack — Python, SQL, Excel/VBA, Power BI, or whatever your team already runs — and deliver into your repositories and environments, not ours.

Which office handles my engagement?

The one nearest your timezone — Dublin, Dubai, or Chittagong. All three run the same process and documentation standard, so the output is identical wherever the work happens.

Can we start small?

That's what the audit is for. It's fixed-price, takes about two weeks, and the fee is credited in full against any project you commission within 90 days.
